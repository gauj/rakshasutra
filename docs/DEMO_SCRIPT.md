# Demo video script (under 3 minutes)

The story: **a parent asks Bee to keep an eye on Adam through a school day.** The video opens with your real Watch, then a time-lapse plays the day from 9 to 6.

Say this plainly once, near the start: **the guardian's Bee is live; the child's location in the time-lapse is a simulation.** Do not say a Bee was worn by the child, or that heart rate was measured.

## Before recording

1. In a PowerShell window: open the project folder, then `npm run demo`. It prints "Bee live: yes".
2. Open **http://localhost:8789** in a browser at 1280x720, dark theme, notifications off. No sign-in, no real data; the places are fictional.
3. Put the Watch on and have a screen recorder that can also record the Watch screen or your voice. Practise the opening sentence once.
4. Rehearse the whole thing once using the buttons below (the time-lapse can be rewound with **↺** or by dragging the slider).
5. Keep off screen: the AWS console, the Set up page, any family code, `data/`.

## Tips

- **Pace:** if the story drags, use the speed buttons (600× or 1200×) or just click the chapter chips; they jump instantly and replay the story up to that moment.
- **Pause:** the **❚❚** button holds the clock while you talk. Drag the slider for any hour.
- **If the live Bee note does not appear** within 10 seconds, say it once more. The Bee plan card only needs to show once; if it still fails, start the time-lapse and say honestly that the live moment was recorded separately.
- **Keep the recording under 3:00.** Judges are not required to watch past three minutes.

## Claims to avoid

- Do not say the child wears a Bee, or that anyone nearby is alerted automatically.
- Do not say heart rate was measured: a phone has none.
- Do not show Adam's real photo, home, school coordinates or a live iPhone track.
